# inbox

An inbox for collecting ideas and to-dos. When the user says "make a note" or "add an idea", append it to ideas.md without asking where.

| Content | When to read |
|---|---|
| [Idea log](ideas.md) | Recording a new idea, reviewing to-dos, changing the status of an entry |
| [Multi-person collaboration agent](001-multi-person-collab-agent.md) | Looking at the expanded content of this idea in detail |
| [News push pipeline](002-news-pipeline.md) | When doing video summaries, daily digests, or scheduling DeepSeek subagents |

Rules:

- New ideas are appended to the end of `ideas.md`, in the format at the top of that file.
- Update the status when there is progress; do not delete entries; for abandoned ones, write one sentence on the reason.
- When an idea enters "planning" and needs expanding, create a separate page in the corresponding area or project, and keep only a link here.
